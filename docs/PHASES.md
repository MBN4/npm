# NMP Phase-by-Phase Roadmap

### Phase 0: Discovery & Architecture — COMPLETE
- Workspace audit, stack selection, living docs creation, and architecture approval.

### Phase 1: Foundation — IN PROGRESS
- Database, Auth, Roles, Permissions, Base UI Layout, Theme, Audit Logging.

### Phase 2: Medicine & Inventory — PENDING
- Master Catalog, Generics, Brands, Barcodes, Batches, Movements, Valuation.

### Phase 3: Suppliers & Purchases — PENDING
- Supplier Ledgers, Purchases, Invoices, Batch Inward, Returns.

### Phase 4: POS & Sales Counter — PENDING
- Barcode/Keyboard POS, FEFO Engine, Expiry Block, Payments, Returns, Receipts.

### Phase 5: Customers, CRM & Prescriptions — PENDING
- Patient CRM, Credit Ledger, Rx Records, Refills.

### Phase 6: Expiry Control & Notifications — PENDING
- 180/90/60/30/7-day Expiry Buckets, Disposal Register, Alerts.

### Phase 7: Accounts & Cashbook — PENDING
- Cashbook, Expenses, Payables, Receivables, P&L.

### Phase 8: Reports & Analytics — PENDING
- Financial and Inventory Reports, PDF/Excel Exports.

### Phase 9: Demand Forecasting Intelligence — PENDING
- Sales velocity, ADC, Days of Stock, Reorder Suggestions.

### Phase 10: Drug AI & Safety Assistant — PENDING
- Verified Clinical DB, AI Interaction Warnings, Alternative Finder.

### Phase 11: Hardware & Utilities — PENDING
- ESC/POS Thermal Printing, Barcode Generator, Excel Bulk Import/Export.

### Phase 12: Backup, Multi-PC & Offline — PENDING
- Hot SQLite Backups, LAN Multi-Terminal Networking, Disaster Recovery.

### Phase 13: Hardening & Security Audit — PENDING
- Concurrency testing, penetration review, performance optimization.

### Phase 14: Final QA & Release — PENDING
- End-to-end regression, user guide, release sign-off.
